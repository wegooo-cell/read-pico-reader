Source: https://github.com/CloudZao/Metalio-E-INK4-Plus

Material used from this project:

- `main/boards/metalio-e-ink4-plus/config.h` - pin map, TCA9555 bit assignments
- `main/boards/metalio-e-ink4-plus/bus/tps65185.{c,h}` - TPS65185 register map, VCOM
  encoding (mV/10 across VCOM2/VCOM1) and the thermistor conversion sequence
- `main/boards/metalio-e-ink4-plus/bus/pca9555.{c,h}` - 9555 access pattern
- `main/boards/metalio-e-ink4-plus/display/epd_board_metalio_eink4_plus.c` - the
  TPS65185 power-on/power-off sequence and its interaction with PWR_GOOD
- `main/boards/metalio-e-ink4-plus/display/epdiy_riscv_ve_stub.c` - the C equivalents
  of the Xtensa SIMD paths, including the ESP32-S31 in-byte pixel order
- `main/boards/metalio-e-ink4-plus/display/output_lcd/{lcd_driver.c,render_lcd.c}` -
  the ESP32-S31 LCD blocks: dedicated LCD_DATA IOMUX, cache-coherent bounce buffers
  and the pixel-clock gate the HAL leaves shut
